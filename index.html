About Manlung Tech City

Manlung Tech City is a premier cyber security and digital services hub based in Nairobi, Kenya. We provide enterprise-grade security tools, government e-service assistance (KRA, eCitizen, E-Certificates, Passport, Good Conduct), and professional training courses. Our mission is to make cyber security accessible, affordable, and effective for individuals, businesses, and government agencies.

We are ISO 27001 compliant and adhere to Kenya's Data Protection Act. All transactions are secured via Paystack with PCI-DSS compliance.

Our team of certified professionals offers 24/7 tech support, system monitoring, and incident response. We combine cutting-edge technology with human expertise to protect your digital assets.
